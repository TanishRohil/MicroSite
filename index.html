    XIMB, Xavier Square

    Xavier Square, Jayadev Vihar, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Stage events run on the main ground and in the Auditorium; competitions in the Exam Hall and classroom blocks. Report at the help desk by the main gate (tentative).

    Open in Maps ↗
    Getting here
    About 20 minutes by cab from Biju Patnaik International Airport and roughly 25 minutes from Bhubaneswar railway station (approx.).
    Stay
    Shared on-campus stay for outstation teams on request, 16 to 19 Jan 2027 (tentative).
    Entry & ID
    Carry a valid college ID. Passes are single-entry: no re-entry once you exit.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    FAQs.

    When will the festival schedule be available?

    A tentative schedule for 17 to 19 January 2027 is under Plan your visit. Final timings are expected by 1 January 2027. Register your interest to hear first.

    Who can participate?

    Competitions are open to college and B-school students from across India. Each event card lists its team size and format.

    Can I participate as a team?

    Team sizes vary by event. Check the event rules once they're published.

    Does registering interest reserve a spot?

    No. Interest submissions only tell us you'd like to participate or attend. Entry and competition registration follow the organisers' confirmed process.

    Is there a fee?

    Fees vary by event and will be listed when registrations open on 1 December 2026 (tentative).

    Can I volunteer?

    Yes. Tick X-Athon or Vriddhi in the interest form and the Social Responsibility Cell will reach out about volunteering.
