    GOOD TO KNOW

    FAQs.

    When will the festival schedule be available?

    Dates and timings will be added under Plan your visit once the organising team confirms them. Register your interest to hear first.

    Who can participate?

    Most competitions are open to students from other colleges and B-schools. Eligibility varies by event and will be listed on each event card.

    Can I participate as a team?

    Team sizes vary by event. Check the event rules once they're published.

    Does registering interest reserve a spot?

    No. Interest submissions only tell us you'd like to participate or attend. Entry and competition registration follow the organisers' confirmed process.

    Is there a fee?

    Fees and deadlines will be shared per event after confirmation.

    Can I volunteer?

    Volunteer roles will be listed under Give Back if confirmed by the organisers. Pick Give back in the interest form to get updates.
